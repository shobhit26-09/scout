Questions

Is Scout really free?

Yes. No account, no trial, no paywall. Scout runs on free infrastructure, so it limits how many audits one visitor can run in ten minutes.

What does it check?

SEO, performance, security, accessibility, crawlability, links, mobile and social previews, plus a Trust verdict. Scroll up to "Nine categories" for the detail on each.

How is the score calculated?

Each category starts at 100. Critical problems take off the most, warnings less, and info notes a little. The overall score is the combined result. It is plain arithmetic, so the same page always gets the same score. Passed checks are listed too.

What does the Trust verdict mean?

It estimates how likely a site is to be a scam. It looks at Google Safe Browsing, how old the domain is, odd or lookalike URLs, and patterns like urgency language or password forms on thin pages. Safe means no signals were found, not a guarantee. Each flag shows its evidence.

Do you store my reports?

Yes. Each audit is saved so it can live at a shareable link, and recent audits are listed on this page. Scout stores the results, not visitor data, and does not use trackers. Do not audit anything you want kept private.

Will it hit my site hard?

No. Scout identifies itself as ScoutBot, follows robots.txt, and fetches one page at a time with a short pause between requests. Whole-site crawls stop at 50 pages.

Can I see how it works?

Scout is open source. Read the code on GitHub.
